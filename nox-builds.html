Nox Composites Builds

Each of our wheels are built by hand to meticulous standards. They are light and strong and come with our best in the industry lifetime warranty. They are also totally customizable with spoke and hub upgrades of your choice.

These are our 4 main Nox build options. We can also custom build a set of road or cross wheels from Nox. Use the custom build option for Nox builds not found here.

Skyline
wheels/nox/skyline.jpg

The Skyline is our dedicated cross country race rim. Fast, stiff and light. learn more...

Teocalli
wheels/nox/teocalli.jpg

The Teocalli is a purpose built cross country rim thats no afraid to go some all mountain. Work without the weight penalty. learn more...

Farlow
wheels/nox/farlow.jpg

The Farlow's beefy design allows for some serious hard work while running wider tires for all day all mountain fun. learn more...

Kitsuma
wheels/nox/kitsuma.jpg

The Kitsuma is a hard charging wide beast allowing for tires up to 3.0 for fat applications or just about anything you want to run them through. learn more...
