Wheel Rebuild & Repair

Bent rim, cracked hub, broken spokes, or a wheel that just won't stay true? Send it my way. I rebuild and repair mountain bike wheels by hand and get them rolling straight again, often for a lot less than the price of a new set.

What I can do

  • Re-tension and true a wheel that's gone out of round
  • Replace broken or damaged spokes
  • Swap a cracked or dented rim and reuse your hub
  • Service, rebuild or replace hubs
  • Full rebuild with upgraded spokes, nipples or hubs
